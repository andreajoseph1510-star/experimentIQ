Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026

ExperimentIQ (experiment-iq.vercel.app) is an A/B testing and statistical analysis tool built by Ann as a student project.

What we collect

When you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, and profile picture. We also store the experiments you choose to save (names, visitor counts, conversion counts, and results).

How we use it

Your Google information is used only to sign you in and to show you your own saved experiments. We do not sell, share, or use your data for advertising.

Where it is stored

Data is stored with Supabase. Each user can only access their own experiments.

Deleting your data

You can delete any saved experiment inside the app. To delete your account and all your data, email the address below and it will be removed.
